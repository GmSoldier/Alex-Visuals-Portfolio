How I work

I use AI to help translate ideas I already have into advertising imagery. I am interested in how composition, visual storytelling and product presentation can influence how a product is perceived, while keeping the concept connected to believable product characteristics.

What I am developing

I am improving my workflow through image editing, composition planning and basic 3D tools so I can have more control over the final result and rely less on random generations.
